Series 06 · 2026

Sea and Light

9 photographs March 2026 Zhuhai

In March 2026 I went to Zhuhai with my team for the China round of a HOSA thirty-second public service advertising competition. The city is built along the coast, one bridge from Macau, and something melancholy runs through all of it. The weather was in on it too — overcast nearly the whole time we were there.

The first afternoon we went to Chimelong, the resort Zhuhai is known for. Between the drops on the roller coasters I kept stopping to photograph. Someone sitting under the aquarium tunnel with their head down while the big fish went over. A shaft of daylight falling through a hole in a dark ceiling, one person standing under it looking up. A lit wall of water with kelp growing in it, and a silhouette in front of it. After dark the drones went up and made a jellyfish out of light, and the fireworks came up red underneath.

The next morning we walked out into the city instead. A white arch framing someone who stood facing an empty road under a flyover. A small red-roofed tower at the edge of the sea, and a person in a straw hat sitting in front of it, looking out.

I only noticed this afterwards: not one person in these nine photographs is facing me. They are all backs and silhouettes, and every one of them is looking at something — the fish, the light, the drones, the sea. I was standing behind them, photographing what they were looking at. In the last frame the sky had finally cleared, and someone stood in front of a huge yellow sculpture with both arms out. Still with their back to me.

Those few days do not feel entirely real to me now. The photographs are the part I am sure of.
