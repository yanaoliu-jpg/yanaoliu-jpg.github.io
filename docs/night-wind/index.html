Drunk on the Night Wind

9 photographs July 2025 Aranya, Beidaihe

In the summer of 2025 I went to Aranya, a resort on the coast at Beidaihe, a few hours from Beijing, with a friend and their family. We went for what everyone goes for: the sand, the waves, the slow kind of day you only get beside the sea.

The photographs I came back with are all from after the sun went down.

I don’t think I decided this. It was just that the place at night was the part I couldn’t stop looking at. An empty restaurant terrace with the ceiling fans still turning. A courtyard lit yellow between two buildings. Two kids at a floodlit skate park, one of them crouched down filming the other. A man walking past a café window with all the stools inside still empty.

None of it is the scenery we came for. It is what the town looks like once the day lets go of it — people still out, still moving at the pace the place was built for. That pace is the thing I actually wanted to keep.
