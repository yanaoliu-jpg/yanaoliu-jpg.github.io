Gratitude, Quietly

5:21 Documentary short November 2025

What gratitude actually means is buried somewhere in an ordinary day.

It is not the ritual you perform once a year. Breakfast from the kitchen staff, the guard at the gate, the cleaners in the corridors, the teachers preparing lessons — small, entirely concrete things that add up to the steadiest part of a school day. Most of us walk past all of it without looking up.

So over Thanksgiving we went and asked the people in each of those jobs what their day is actually like.

The kitchen manager said he clocks in first thing, then goes through room by room checking whether the hygiene passes. The cleaner said her day is simply cleaning — and then added that when a student says “you’ve worked hard today,” it stays with her for the rest of it. The guard at the gate said he can only clock off once nearly everyone has gone home. The teachers talked about what passes between them and their students.

Five minutes, a team of eight, and me on camera.

The film is in Mandarin with Chinese subtitles.
