Good Night

9 photographs November – December 2023 Beijing

These photographs were made on four evenings between November and December of 2023, in the two places in Beijing I knew best: the campus of RDFZ Branch School, where I went to middle school, and Yicheng Dongyuan, the compound where I live.

The first three come from a break between evening study sessions. I had brought my camera to school on purpose that day — a classmate’s birthday, and I was the one taking the pictures. When the break came I carried it out to the yard instead. I had crossed that yard every day for three years and had never once seen it after dark. The track was empty except for one person still running.

The rest are from December, after the first real snow — the one that means winter has officially arrived. I was shooting from an unlit room, and what I kept photographing turned out to be windows. From the dark you can see a hundred lit ones at once. I kept wondering what each of those households was doing at that exact moment, and whether their thoughts had drifted out of the city along with the snow.

I’ve come to think a camera gives you more than photographs. It gives you a way of remembering a season — of what the weather was doing while you were living inside it.
