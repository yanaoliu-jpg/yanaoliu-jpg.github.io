Stop Scrolling, Stay Alive

32 seconds National Outstanding Honor Public Service Announcement HOSA 2026 March 2026

Thirty seconds on what an algorithmic feed does to a teenager, and on getting out of it.

It opens with numbers on screen: three hours of screen time, work efficiency down forty percent, prefrontal cortex activity down thirty-seven. Then the boy at the desk, the boy at dinner with his friends around him and his face in his phone — scenes pass, stay stuck. Red words drift across his exam papers: anxiety, depression, myopia, grades dropping. Then he puts the phone down and picks up a pen. Break the loop. The last shot is a table of people raising their glasses.

We had thirty seconds, so every cut had to carry something. The team went over the pacing again and again — how long to hold on the phone before it starts to feel wrong, where the type comes in, when the music turns. I shot it.

It was made in March 2026 and took a National Outstanding Honor at HOSA that year.
