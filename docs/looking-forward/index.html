Series 06 · 2026

Looking Forward

9 photographs March 2026 Tung Chung, Hong Kong

In March 2026 I went to Hong Kong on my own to sit the SAT.

March in Hong Kong has not turned hot yet. There was still some winter left in the wind, and the sun was warm without any heat in it. I stayed with a colleague of my mother’s, in Tung Chung — an enormous residential estate where footbridges run from one tower to the next and on into the malls. At either end of the working day those bridges and malls are full of people.

What I came back with is almost always whoever was alone in it. A child crossing an empty plaza. Two girls walking home from school. Someone carrying a plastic bag up a flight of steps. A small child in a helmet cycling past a lit Thai restaurant. Near dark, one more person cutting across the brick paving with their shopping, a delivery truck just stopped behind them.

When the light went I photographed the room I was staying in — unlit, the curtains half drawn, hills outside the window. The next afternoon: a row of empty benches with the azaleas out. And then the airport, a line of aircraft behind the glass with one of them already taxiing away.

I am keeping these so that later I can come back to those two days.
