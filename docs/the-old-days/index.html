The Old Days

9 photographs May 2025 Beijing

I went back on an afternoon in May 2025, a few days after my AP exams, carrying a camera. This is the middle school I came from, and everything in these photographs was still exactly where I had left it a year before — which made the whole place feel like two things at once, a little dreamlike and completely real.

Walking through it, I kept feeling that what had happened in those rooms, and the people I had known there, were only a day old. The late sun was coming into every corner of the campus, and it brought back things I had not thought about in a long time.

The last thing I did was look back at the school gate. It is not much to look at, and it opens on the same schedule every morning. But that is where my middle school years stop, and where they stay.
