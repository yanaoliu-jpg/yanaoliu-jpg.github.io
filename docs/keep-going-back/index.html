I Keep Going Back

9 photographs July – August 2025 Shanghai

In late July 2025 I went to Shanghai for a summer program. We stayed at an international school a long way out from the center of the city — far enough that the noise stopped, and close enough to the coast that at night the wind still came in off the water. Three days, our schedule full the whole time, and almost none of what we were officially there for is in these pictures.

The first evening I found a hall with thousands of small glass spheres hanging from the ceiling and four people sitting on the floor underneath them, talking. The next afternoon the campus was empty: a road running straight out between two rows of trees, a track with one runner on the far side of it. Then it rained, and I photographed the city through a window I could not properly see through.

On the last day we went into town, to a film museum. Someone stood a long time in front of a lit wall of old movie posters. Someone leaned in over a row of old movie cameras and stacked reels of film. Someone in a backwards cap reached out to spin a zoetrope and watch the little red and blue figures start to run.

I only noticed this afterwards: not one person in these nine photographs is looking at the camera. They are all looking at something else, and I am behind a window, or behind the glass of a case, or on the other side of a running track. On the last night someone sat down on the floor with a guitar and about twenty of us gathered around, nearly all of them holding up a phone. I was at the back holding a camera instead. That is the one I keep going back to.
