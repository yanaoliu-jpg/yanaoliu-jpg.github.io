Make a Wish

10:11 July 2026

Everyone is carrying a wish somewhere — however small, however ordinary, it still deserves to be heard.

On an afternoon in July we sat on a bench in a residential courtyard and talked for a long time with a woman who cleans other people’s homes for a living. One job is three hours; some days she does several. Her eyes get so tired she can hardly keep them open. The walk home is the most relaxed part of her day. When there is time, she cooks something she actually likes.

Listening to her, we realised she spends almost all of her hours looking after other people’s lives, and is almost never asked what she wants.

“Make a wish” is usually a weightless thing to say to someone. This time we wanted it to land. After the interview we quietly put a present together, and took her wish one step further.

Her wish was a small one — small enough that nobody normally thinks to ask. But being heard properly, and answered properly, may have mattered more than the present did.

The film is in Mandarin with Chinese subtitles.
